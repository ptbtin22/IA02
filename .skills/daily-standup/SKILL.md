# Daily Standup Workflow
1. Call the `list_tasks` tool on your MCP server to collect open and completed tasks.
2. Organize the tasks into three structured sections:
   - **Done**: List completed tasks (marked as ✅).
   - **Today**: List open tasks to complete today (marked as ⬜).
   - **Blockers**: List any blockers or write "None".
3. Keep the output under 10 lines, concise, with bullet points and no fluff.
